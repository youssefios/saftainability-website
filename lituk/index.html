Life in the UK: Animated

The Life in the UK handbook, acted out in short animated lessons you will actually remember.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

What is inside

  • 43 animated lessons, about 135 minutes in all, across every chapter of the handbook
  • Narration with captions that light up each word as it is spoken
  • 813 practice questions, random mock tests timed like the real test, and 33 fixed practice tests
  • Flip cards for dates, people, numbers and common traps
  • A timeline of 96 key dates on one page
  • Works fully offline. No account, no adverts, no tracking

Unofficial study aid. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the Home Office or the UK Government. The lessons follow the official handbook, Life in the United Kingdom: A Guide for New Residents. For official test information and booking, go to gov.uk/life-in-the-uk-test.

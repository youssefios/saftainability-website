Support

Need help or found a mistake in a lesson? Email support@saftainability.com with your phone model and the lesson or question number. We read every message.

What the app covers

43 short animated lessons across the chapters of the official handbook, Life in the United Kingdom: A Guide for New Residents, plus 813 practice questions, timed mock tests, flip cards, a numbers page and a timeline of key dates.

For the official test details and booking, go to gov.uk/life-in-the-uk-test.

Common questions

Do I need the internet? No. Everything is inside the app.

Where is my progress saved? On your phone only. There is no account, so progress does not move between devices.

I cannot hear the narration. Check the volume, then reopen the lesson. On iPhone, also check the ring/silent switch.

Captions are too small. Tap the CC button during a lesson to switch to large captions.

Refunds. Purchases are handled by the store you bought from. iPhone: reportaproblem.apple.com. Android: Google Play refunds.
